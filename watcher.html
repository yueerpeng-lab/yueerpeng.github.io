Watcher — Consumer Subtitle Review

The consumer-facing companion to HAMM. Watcher lets reviewers select a title, upload a video, and produce AI-generated subtitles ready for download — a streamlined flow built for non-technical users contributing to Disney's localisation pipeline.

Role

Product Intern · UI Design + QA

Type

Consumer SaaS · Material Web App

Period

Feb – Jul 2025

Tools

Figma · Jira · MagicPod

01 / Overview

Localisation, but for everyone.

Disney's subtitle pipeline isn't only for production professionals. Reviewers, consultants, and external partners also need to validate AI-generated subtitles — ideally without learning a complex enterprise platform.

Watcher is the entry point for that audience. A familiar Material Design language, single-purpose forms, and a clear primary action keep the interaction approachable.

Selected Screens

Inside the platform

空状态 (Empty State) 极简的入口设计，引导用户通过核心动作开始字幕审核流程。 A minimal entry design that guides users to start the subtitle review process through core actions.
项目列表 (Project List) 任务状态一目了然，方便用户快速定位需要处理的翻译或审核工作。 Task statuses are clear at a glance, helping users quickly locate translation or review work.
元数据录入 (Metadata Entry) 流程化的表单设计，确保项目关键信息在上传初期即被准确捕获。 Streamlined form design ensures that key project information is accurately captured during the initial upload.
字幕审核 (Subtitle Review) 实时预览 AI 生成的字幕，支持非技术用户进行交互式的文本纠错与时间轴校验。 Real-time preview of AI-generated subtitles, supporting interactive text correction and timeline validation for non-technical users.
配音流程 (Dubbing Flow) 简化的多语言轨道管理，让繁琐的音视频同步工作变得直观易操作。 Simplified multi-language track management makes tedious audio-video synchronization intuitive and easy to operate.

02 / Reflection

Designing for second-time users.

Most enterprise tools optimise for the power user. Watcher had to optimise for someone who'd opened it twice in their life and might not return for weeks. That meant fighting against complexity at every step.

Working alongside HAMM gave me a comparative perspective on dark vs. light tools, enterprise vs. consumer flows, and the consequential differences in density and rhythm.
