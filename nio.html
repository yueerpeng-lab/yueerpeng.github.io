NIO House — User Community & Events

NIO House is an open, welcoming space for our users and community to express, share, and experience memorable moments together. As one of the NIO staff members, my role was to create endless possibilities for the community through curated programming and events.

Role

Type

Period

Audience

NIO owners · Their families

01 / Project Overview

An open space for memorable moments.

NIO House is an open, welcoming space for our users and community to express, share, and experience memorable moments together. As a NIO staff member, my goal was to design and execute physical events that create endless possibilities for our community, turning an empty space into an inhabited, vibrant hub.

Event Programming

Building Connections

For Adults

We held workshops including reading, sharing, and handicrafts. These programmes were designed around shared craft and conversation, allowing users to connect organically.

For Children

We held special events for children to build relations. The intent was building intergenerational bonds within the community and creating a joyful, family-friendly environment.

03 / Reflection

Loyalty is built in shared rooms.

Working at NIO House gave me a perspective on user retention that has stayed with me through every product role since: digital touchpoints can move metrics, but the retention that actually compounds is built in shared physical and emotional space.

The community frame — designing the conditions for people to encounter each other meaningfully — has informed how I think about onboarding, empty states, and even subtle community signals in pure-digital products.
